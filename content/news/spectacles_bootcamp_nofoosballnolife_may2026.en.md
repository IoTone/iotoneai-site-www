We are thrilled to share that IoTone Japan, in collaboration with Pangolin Interactive, has been featured by Snap's Spectacles team for **No Foosball No Life**, our multiplayer AR foosball experience. We were also honored to be one of just **50 developers worldwide** invited to the Spectacles Bootcamp.

## Recognition from Spectacles

> "We were thrilled to have you! The energy at the bootcamp was incredible, and this collaboration is exactly the kind of innovation we love to see." — Spectacles team

The original project dropped at Lensfest in February 2026. Getting recognized by a major publicly traded social media network and publisher of original content means a lot to our small team in Fukuoka.

## About No Foosball No Life

No Foosball No Life explores the boundaries of hand-tracking and multiplayer synchronization on Snap Spectacles. The project showcases the versatility of **SyncKit**, offering:

- **1P solo play** against a sophisticated AI opponent
- **4-player co-op modes** where players must physically share the rods

It's classic tabletop foosball reimagined for spatial AR.
