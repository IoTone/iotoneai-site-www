Registration is now open for **XR VisionDevCamp Fukuoka 2026**, a 2.5-day spatial-computing hackathon taking place **June 26–28, 2026** at **Engineer Café in CIC Fukuoka (ONE FUKUOKA BLDG.)**. IoTone Japan is proud to return as a general sponsor.

- Event site: [https://www.xrvdc.org](https://www.xrvdc.org)
- Register: [https://luma.com/i5gerreb](https://luma.com/i5gerreb)

## Hackathon Overview

XR VisionDevCamp continues nearly two decades of hackathon heritage — from iPhoneDevCamp (2007–2009) through VisionDevCamp (2024–2025) to this expanded 2026 edition spanning the full XR spectrum.

- **Collaboration over competition.** Teams share code and knowledge.
- **All ages and experience levels welcome.** No qualification gate; beginners get in-event learning sessions and active staff support for team formation.
- **Bilingual.** Japanese and English spoken throughout the event.
- **Teams of 1–6** (solo entries welcome). Participants may join multiple teams.
- **Loaner hardware provided** during the event.

Developers build for the full spatial-computing lineup — **Apple Vision Pro, Android XR, Meta Quest, Snap Spectacles, Xreal, and HTC** devices.

## Format

The weekend culminates in a hackathon, AR Expo, and Closing Awards. Judging is intentionally flexible — judges may invent new categories on the spot if a project deserves recognition. Winners receive social media validation, publicity, and small monetary prizes.
