We are excited to announce **XR VisionDevCamp 2026**, the next chapter of the VisionDevCamp series. Following the success of VisionDevCamp 2025, the event has expanded its scope to embrace the full spectrum of XR — AR, VR, and mixed reality — and now welcomes developers, designers, and creators from around the world.

## Event Site

- English: [https://xrvdc.pages.dev/](https://xrvdc.pages.dev/)
- 日本語: [https://xrvdc.pages.dev/ja](https://xrvdc.pages.dev/ja)

## Get Involved

Whether you are an experienced XR developer or just getting started, XR VisionDevCamp is a place to build, collaborate, and ship something memorable. Reach out if you'd like to participate, sponsor, mentor, or volunteer.

IoTone Japan is proud to continue supporting the international XR hackathon community.
